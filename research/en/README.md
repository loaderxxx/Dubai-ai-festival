## Reading relationships correctly

- **Documented product connection:** an official source names a connector, integration or ecosystem entry. The specific product and its limits matter; this does not automatically prove a partnership.
- **Named alliance or partner statement:** an official vendor names another organization in this role. It is not proof of a technical connector, contract terms or a successful deployment.
- **Complementarity hypothesis:** published capabilities suggest a possible combination. Feasibility, business value and both parties' interest still need validation.
- **Competitive overlap:** companies solve part of the same problem. This calls for comparison, not a claim that their products are equivalent.
