## Meet LYRA: private by design

LYRA is a private personal operating system developed around its owner’s work and creative life. It is a closed system, not a publicly available product and not currently for sale. This page introduces the idea, not a signup or product launch.

From music to research, LYRA helps connect creative work with practical action:

- **Music and creative work:** developing concepts, lyrics and production directions, working with creative tools.
- **Research:** finding sources, comparing companies and products, structuring evidence into an Atlas.
- **Organisation:** preparing plans, messages, summaries and next steps.
- **AI workflows and engineering:** helping design, configure and improve tool-assisted processes.

These are workflows carried out with tools, available permissions and human collaboration—not a claim that one model can do everything independently. Outputs need review; important decisions remain with the person.

**Our attitude:** if we do not yet know how to do something, we explore it, build a workflow and test it. Curiosity is a commitment to learn, not a promise of unlimited capability.

## Ten named assistants and agents

The following descriptions summarise official vendor claims, checked on 26 October 2026. We have not independently tested these products. Language coverage, pricing, service guarantees and live availability are not asserted unless specifically supported below.

### Kira · KIRA AI

A voice-first personal assistant for memory, calendars, email and workflows. The closest conceptual neighbour here to a personal operating system.

**Interface / status / evidence:** Voice-first; official site describes life operating software.

[Official product & source](https://kiraai.ai/)

### VERA · Planytics AI

Virtual Enterprise Resolution Agent: supply-chain planning, inventory and coordination of operational agents. The focus is enterprise operations rather than a general-purpose personal companion.

**Interface / status / evidence:** Named enterprise agent; site describes reading, reasoning and resolving.

[Official product & source](https://planytics.ai/)

### Ari · Life Intelligence Group

A personal AI chief of staff for planning, organisation and delegation to specialist agents. The interesting pattern is one coordinating assistant backed by a team of specialists.

**Interface / status / evidence:** Product site names Ari and its parent company; no hands-on availability check.

[Official product & source](https://askariapp.com/)

### Ava · Artisan AI

An AI business-development representative for prospecting, outbound communication and meeting booking. A specialist sales workflow, not a universal assistant.

**Interface / status / evidence:** Officially presented as an AI BDR; the related Artie family is listed separately below.

[Official product & source](https://www.artisan.co/ai-sales-agent)

### Athena · Audit Sense

A financial-analysis and audit-question assistant: questions over spreadsheets, anomaly investigation and financial data exploration.

**Interface / status / evidence:** Ask Athena; Excel/CSV question-and-answer workflow. Not an independently validated audit opinion.

[Official product & source](https://auditsense.ai/ask-athena/)

### Amira · Amira AI

Customer operations across calls, WhatsApp, email and chat, with handover to people. The name belongs to an omnichannel customer-operations system, not necessarily one personal character.

**Interface / status / evidence:** Calls, messaging, email and chat; human handover is advertised.

[Official product & source](https://amira-ai.com/Home)

### Rova · Provance

An integrated-facility-management agent: monitoring, task assignment and operational coordination for buildings and facilities.

**Interface / status / evidence:** The official PROAI page introduces Rova as its IFM agent.

[Official product & source](https://www.provance.ai/proai)

### Hadi · Proptech AI

A real-estate and local-lifestyle assistant covering property, neighbourhood and rental questions. The vendor gives this agent a camel mascot and a personal name.

**Interface / status / evidence:** WhatsApp-based interaction is advertised.

[Official product & source](https://proptechai.ae/hadi)

### Remo · Remo AI

A project-scoped property-development assistant for concepts, procurement and construction schedules. Useful to distinguish project context from a generic real-estate chatbot.

**Interface / status / evidence:** REMO Chat Agent — Beta. Official search-index evidence was readable; direct homepage access failed during research.

[Official product & source](https://byremo.ai/assistant)

### Abigail · Abigail AI

A patent-prosecution assistant for applications, prior-art research and responses to patent-office actions. Its context is professional patent work, not general legal advice.

**Interface / status / evidence:** Ask Abigail; lawyer-directed assistance, not a replacement for counsel.

[Official product & source](https://abigail.app/)
