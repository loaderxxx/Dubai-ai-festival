This is research sequencing by the requested tiers, **not** a private sales ranking. Before each sponsor profile: verify the brand–domain–legal-entity chain and current festival role, then products, buyers, evidence, governance and commercial access. Prices, trials, client results, interfaces and resale rights remain unverified until the individual profile is researched.
