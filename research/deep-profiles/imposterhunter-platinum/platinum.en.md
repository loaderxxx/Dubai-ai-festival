Five focused questions for product/commercial discovery:

1. Which entity contracts for each product/market, and which owner approves integration or delivery work?
2. For one proposed workflow, which native connector operations are supported, and can the team supply versioned API/auth/error/rate-limit documentation and authorized test access?
3. Where do that workflow's content, embeddings, inference, recordings and logs go, and how do local-first claims apply to each?
4. What action-specific approval, pause, revocation and deletion evidence can be demonstrated in the agreed configuration?
5. What written example explains seat minimums, pooled minutes, tokens/credits, support responsibilities and any permitted channel arrangement?

**INFERENCE:** these are qualification gates, not findings that the product is defective. A public tier listing establishes neither reseller rights nor unrestricted access. No accounts, installations, transactions, forms or outbound messages were used.
