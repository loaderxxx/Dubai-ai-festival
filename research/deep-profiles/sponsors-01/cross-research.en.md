## Order of validation

First reconcile Kira's public documentation and Integra's current tier/scope: evidence can be checked without client data, although budget and owner interest remain unknown. Next identify whether a single Integra or Campus implementation task has an unmet delivery gap; these organizations already offer substantial delivery. KB requires subsidiary and sponsor selection before designing a bank pilot. Cross-company event workflows and Kira enterprise integrations follow only after supported interfaces, permissions and existing-tool overlap are understood.

This is qualitative research triage using evidence, unmet-need uncertainty, feasibility, access burden and risk. It estimates neither deal probability nor revenue. Personal commercial priorities and pilot effort remain private.
