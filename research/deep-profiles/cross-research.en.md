## Validation order

First reconcile publicly visible catalogue/date inconsistencies: they can be examined with public data and clear acceptance criteria. Next test whether training participants need bounded implementation support; buyer demand is still unknown. Cloud-benefit activation follows only after current programme eligibility is confirmed. Event CRM and voice hypotheses require higher access and privacy effort and should follow an explicit owner, approved exports and confirmation that the existing stack does not already solve the problem.

This order weighs evidence availability, unconfirmed buyer pain, feasibility, access burden and risk qualitatively. It predicts neither deal probability nor revenue. Exact pilot effort and personal commercial priorities are outside this public atlas.
