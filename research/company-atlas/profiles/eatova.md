## English

Scans restaurant menus against a user dietary profile and helps formulate questions for staff and translate dietary needs.

**Integration:** Photo, QR and link inputs are documented. No restaurant POS integration or public enterprise API verified.

**Collaboration hypothesis, not an agreement:** Hypothesis: test menu clarity with a hospitality partner, keeping staff confirmation mandatory and avoiding food-safety guarantees.
