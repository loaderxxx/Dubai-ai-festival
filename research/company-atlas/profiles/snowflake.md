## English

Snowflake combines enterprise data with AI services; Cortex Search supplies hybrid retrieval and reranking.

**Integration:** Atlan has a documented Snowflake connector. Cortex Search exposes a governed service, but searches run with owner’s rights.

**Collaboration hypothesis, not an agreement:** Scope an implementation or ecosystem collaboration after resolving these procurement questions: Which data may the service owner read, and how will caller-specific restrictions be enforced and tested?
