## How to read and verify

- Each profile covers capabilities, limitations, evidence and questions for the next conversation; prospective collaboration remains a hypothesis.
- Six structured dossiers include separate service and alternative matrices. Planytics is a standalone deep dive with an evidence supplement, not the same dossier template.
- First-pass briefs and the original 18-company study are not counted among these seven deep studies. Do not add coverage figures from different research layers.
- Private contacts, correspondence and personal commercial notes are excluded.
