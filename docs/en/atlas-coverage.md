## Definitions and provenance

- Company universe: the original public floorplan capture at **03:30:39 UTC**, 264 rows → 262 labels → 248 candidate organization labels. Shared stands and co-brands do not prove legal identity.
- Speaker/agenda source responses: **13:18 UTC**, official public directory, both day filters and startup pitch table. [Extraction, response hashes, conflicts and gaps](../../research/speakers/coverage.json).
- Sponsor role denominator: the official **2026 Sponsors** page, not the smaller homepage logo subset. [Roster boundaries and contact caveats](../../research/organizers-sponsors/COVERAGE.md).
- Company briefs have individual sources, review dates, identity confidence and research depth. [Coverage JSON](../../research/company-atlas/coverage.json) · [Source register](../../research/company-atlas/sources.json).
- All proposed collaborations are questions or hypotheses. Advertised integrations are not runtime tests. No test or client outcome is fabricated.

## Known gaps worth keeping visible

1. **All 248 labels were investigated;58 company identities remain unresolved and therefore floorplan-only.** The atlas covers their existence in the captured plan, not their offering. Resolve names against official sources before commercial recommendations.
2. The official agenda and startup page disagree over the dates of the Canva workshop and AI-agent championship; a published time typo and one startup-presenter name conflict remain unresolved.
3. No recording/transcript corpus or actual-talk reporting has been established. **27 October is future relative to this snapshot.**
4. Public contact routes vary in purpose and source age; in particular AI Office, Mantika, Kira and KB caveats remain in the directory. No email deliverability or contact responsiveness was tested.
5. Sources may change after capture. External links are evidence pointers, not guarantees of continued availability.

## Public/private boundary

Only public official company, event and professional information is included. Private attendee exports, private conversations, personal meeting notes and internal project architecture are excluded. The pre-existing owner-approved private-archive **aggregate** is not a public contacts list. No outreach was sent.

## How to use this responsibly

Start with a company record → read its sources and status → inspect a related speaker/topic → formulate one testable collaboration question → verify product access, geography, commercial terms and the correct recipient team directly. Reuse the sources and limitations alongside any matrix excerpt. Do not present this independent atlas as an organizer endorsement.
