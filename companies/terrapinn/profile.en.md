The brochure requires contact details and promises email delivery, so it was not obtained. Tier prices, custom work, exclusivity, guaranteed attendance and refund terms are **UNKNOWN**. **INFERENCE.** Treat conference access, exhibition logistics, sponsor activation, digital networking and continuing publishing as different purchase categories. Scope them separately: floor presence is not a promise of qualified demand; a speaking opportunity is not customer procurement; access to a participant interface is not a data licence. The accompanying matrix separates buyer, user, deployment and evidence status. No public self-hosted Terrapinn event product, SDK trial or implementation price was verified.

## C. Business model and procurement

**INFERENCE.** The visible model combines per-event attendance, exhibition packages and negotiated commercial activation. An event owner may also buy managed delivery, but the festival's underlying management agreement and fee basis are **UNKNOWN**. Do not substitute a SaaS/usage model for this evidence or estimate revenue and margin from logos.

The procurement unit should be a specific event, package, dates and accepted order. Ask who owns venue coordination, speaker permissions, content production, app support and post-event reporting. For sponsors, distinguish “access enabled” from “meetings accepted” and “lead received” from “qualified opportunity.” These definitions determine useful acceptance criteria and prevent ambiguous ROI reporting.

**FACT.** General website terms identify Terrapinn Holdings Limited and say specialised terms take precedence. They therefore do not settle every festival order, cancellation condition or data arrangement. Obtain the event order form and applicable schedules before relying on group wording. [T10](https://www.terrapinn.com/terms-and-conditions)

## D. Technical map and integration limits

**FACT:** the iOS listing names **Terrapinn Holdings Pty Ltd** as seller, describes networking/agenda/floor-plan/exhibitor functions and links to DIFC privacy information. Listed interface languages include English, Arabic and Russian; this does not establish translated sessions or Russian-market eligibility. The Android indexed package contains `com.swapcard`; direct page retrieval failed. [T14](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dubai-ai-festival-2026/id6759493882) · [T15](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en&id=com.swapcard.apps.android.networkingdubaiaifestivalcom)

**INFERENCE:** Swapcard is a plausible platform dependency. Terrapinn explicitly references Swapcard for another event, which strengthens the hypothesis without proving this festival's configuration or contract. [T31](https://terrapinn.com/exhibition/highways-th/Event-App.stm)

**FACT, platform documentation:** Swapcard publishes Content, Analytics and Leads APIs plus webhooks. Organizer access uses a permissioned token; exhibitor access uses a booth-specific Exhibitor Center key. These are different authorization contexts. [T16](https://swapcard.dev/) · [T17](https://swapcard.dev/organizer/authentication) · [T18](https://swapcard.dev/exhibitors/authentication)

**UNKNOWN:** Dubai AI Festival enablement, quotas, exports, SSO, account provisioning, CRM connector selection, SDK support, hosting region and monitoring access. Generic platform documentation does not grant an attendee or external consultant permission. Built-in connectors already exist at platform level; first compare them with the requested integration. [T19](https://help.swapcard.com/en/articles/8158956-building-custom-integrations-with-swapcard-s-open-api)

**PROPOSAL:** begin with an approved schema and synthetic records, then a narrowly scoped organiser-supplied export. Only after written authorization consider event keys. Keep credentials server-side, restrict fields, reconcile duplicates, log transformations without sensitive payloads and require staff approval before communication. No login, endpoint test, interception or reverse engineering occurred.

## E. Buyers and concrete use cases

The roles and success conditions below are **INFERENCE**, not named customer contracts.

| Use case | Decision-maker / champion | Success condition | Poor fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise buyer scouting | Innovation/procurement leader / technical evaluator | Predefined requirements; consented introductions; documented follow-up | Buying a pass to obtain unrestricted contacts |
| Startup market discovery | Founder / business development | A small target list; honest product readiness; learning captured after meetings | Expecting a stand to validate product-market fit |
| Sponsor activation | Marketing director / field marketer | Agreed lead definitions and follow-up owner; attribution window | Guaranteed revenue from impressions or logos |
| Institutional programme delivery | Event owner / production lead | Responsibility matrix, approved content and operating timetable | Unbounded ownership across several organisations |
| Post-event evidence review | Commercial director / operations analyst | Reconciled, permitted records and a metric dictionary | Combining all participant data without a defined purpose |

For each, distinguish the person paying from the person operating the tools. Procurement may approve a sponsor order while sales staff need separate app permissions. A measurable operational experiment is more defensible than a broad promise to “improve networking.”
