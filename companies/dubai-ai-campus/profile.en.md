## A. Executive brief and identity

**FACT.** Dubai AI Campus is a **DIFC initiative** in Dubai, UAE; the public business route gives Innovation One, DIFC as its office. The brand's official site is dubaiaicampus.com. A separate incorporated legal entity and registration number for the Campus brand were not established in this review; the contracting entity must be checked for each service. [S01](https://dubaiaicampus.com/blog/announcement/dghr-collaborates-with-dubai-ai-campus-to-accelerate-ai-driven-workforce-development) [S02](https://dubaiaicampus.com/get-in-touch)

**FACT.** Its festival role is **organiser**: DIFC's 20 October 2026 announcement identifies Dubai AI Campus at DIFC as organiser of the third Dubai AI Festival, 26–27 October at Dubai World Trade Centre, in partnership with the UAE AI Office. The festival's About page corroborates the organiser. This is distinct from a sponsor tier or event-management supplier. [S03](https://www.difc.com/whats-on/news/dubai-ai-festival-2026-global-collaboration) [S04](https://dubaiaifestival.com/about-us/)

**INFERENCE.** Its main buyers are founders establishing AI firms, employers buying AI education, and corporate innovation teams seeking ecosystem access. It belongs primarily in **ecosystem and distribution**, with infrastructure access, education and innovation services alongside them. Treating it as a packaged agent platform would produce the wrong technical and procurement questions.

Five verified facts:

1. DIFC publishes an AI commercial licence at **USD 1,500/year**. [S05](https://www.difc.com/business/establish-a-business/ai-licence)
2. A separate DIFC offer is restricted to **new registrants** and itemises coworking and registration. [S06](https://landing.difc.ae/ai-licence-offer)
3. The Campus lists coworking and bespoke offices as separate enquiries. [S07](https://dubaiaicampus.com/coworking-spaces) [S08](https://dubaiaicampus.com/bespoke-offices)
4. Its Academy names Minerva and Udacity education relationships. [S09](https://dubaiaicampus.com/the-campus)
5. Nestlé publicly announced an AI product-innovation collaboration in February 2025. [S10](https://www.nestle-mena.com/en/media/pressreleases/allpressreleases/nestle-collaborates-with-dubai-ai-campus)

Five decision-critical unknowns: complete incorporation and renewal cost; current eligibility and expiry of incentives; available delivery opportunities for external specialists; programme-specific privacy and IP terms; measured production outcomes of corporate collaborations. These gaps prevent a procurement-ready endorsement, but do not prevent a focused discovery discussion.

## C. Business model and purchasing

**FACT.** The observable units are annual licensing, workspace charges and course enrolments; registration is an approval process rather than instant SaaS activation. [S05](https://www.difc.com/business/establish-a-business/ai-licence) [S06](https://landing.difc.ae/ai-licence-offer)

**INFERENCE.** Founders are likely economic buyers for establishment; HR/L&D controls training budgets; corporate innovation or transformation functions sponsor customised programmes. Procurement should split licence, lease, education and implementation into separate scopes. Ask who invoices, whether third-party tuition terms apply, and what cancellation, substitution, renewal and IP provisions govern each order. Revenue, margin, referral rates and implementation day rates are UNKNOWN. Investor introductions should be evaluated as access, not a financing commitment.

## D. Technical map and evidence boundary

**VENDOR CLAIM.** DIFC advertises access to AWS and Google startup credits, subsidised Oracle infrastructure, HP GPU hardware, NVIDIA DGX-powered computing and du telecommunications. This supports a benefits/access proposition; it does not prove a unified cloud API, guaranteed allocation, UAE-only processing, benchmark performance or unrestricted commercial entitlements. Confirm provider approval, credit expiry, quotas, account ownership and deployment region separately. [S06](https://landing.difc.ae/ai-licence-offer)

**FACT.** The 29 August 2024 Google Cloud announcement describes selected-startup mentorship and intended Gen AI Startup School activity. **UNKNOWN:** the current intake and specific benefits available to a new applicant in October 2026. [S20](https://www.difc.com/whats-on/news/dubai-ai-campus-and-google-cloud-announce-collaboration)

**UNKNOWN / not located in reviewed pages:** Campus developer API, SDK, connector catalogue, SSO specification, role model, data export format, model routing, agent approval engine, observability interface and service uptime commitment. This is a research limitation, not a claim that no internal systems exist. Documented learning delivery through Forum or external providers should not be conflated with an enterprise AI runtime. Before any integration, obtain a named system owner, authorised documentation and a sandbox with synthetic data. No systems were probed.

## G. Security, privacy and governance

**FACT.** Campus's Privacy Policy link routes to DIFC's policies page, which lists online data protection and information security policies. This is policy-level evidence. It is not a programme-specific DPA or evidence that every resident/provider shares one certification boundary. [S21](https://www.difc.com/our-policies)

**UNKNOWN:** retention periods for application and learning data; model-training opt-out; subprocessors; learner-data hosting regions; tenant segregation; technical incident notification SLA; available audit evidence; certificate scope and validity for a particular service. Ask counsel and procurement to identify controller/processor roles and cross-border transfers. For corporate pilots, require approved data categories, least privilege, access revocation, evaluation logs, human sign-off and deletion evidence. These are proposed controls, not findings of noncompliance. The commercial licence alone should not be treated as permission for every regulated activity; the Campus's activity descriptions contain activity-specific limits. ## I. Improvement hypotheses

All entries are **PROPOSAL**; the underlying problem still needs confirmation with users and service owners.

1. **Programme freshness:** observed Campus/Academy cohort and price mismatch → possible applicant confusion → one versioned catalogue and explicit cohort IDs → reconcile an approved sample of ten listings → requires both content owners → target zero contradictory active fees/dates.
2. **Cost transparency:** licence and promotional line items span pages → possible incomplete budgeting → an eligibility-aware estimate with exclusions and renewal scenarios → compare ten written quotes to displayed assumptions → finance/legal approval → target no unexplained mandatory line item.
3. **Post-training implementation:** programmes already teach roadmaps and governance → verify whether execution support is requested → offer an optional bounded engineering clinic, preserving existing teaching → assess opt-in projects before/after → training partner consent and customer data approval → measure time to first approved pilot and support hours.
4. **Benefits activation:** cloud/hardware access is already advertised → test whether eligible founders struggle with provider hand-offs → a checklist with owners and evidence of activation → follow a consented small cohort → provider programme authorisation → measure activation cycle time and unresolved eligibility questions, without promising credit awards.
