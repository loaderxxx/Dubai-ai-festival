## G. Security, privacy and procurement blockers

**VENDOR CLAIM:** published controls include **TLS 1.2+**, **AES-256**, role controls, access logging and AWS hosting. Framework alignment is not a completed certificate: SOC 2 remains in progress and ISO 27001 planned; “GDPR compliant” is a supplier assertion. No assurance report scope, auditor or validity period was inspected. [KI10](https://kiraai.ai/legal/security-compliance/)

**FACT about disclosures:** the subprocessor table names AWS, Baseten for chat, OpenAI for email embeddings, ElevenLabs for speech and Twilio for telephony, among others. It distinguishes official WhatsApp Business API from optional personal-account companion linking. This establishes disclosed dependencies, not independently confirmed vendor contracts. [KI10](https://kiraai.ai/legal/security-compliance/)

Three conflicts require written clarification:

- **Architecture:** integrations says on-device/zero-knowledge/no storage, while regional and AWS pages describe cloud storage and inference possibly outside the chosen region. Map each feature and data class rather than applying either slogan universally. UAE-local processing is unconfirmed. [KI20](https://kiraai.ai/integrations/) · [KI24](https://kiraai.ai/platform/regional-compliance/) · [KI25](https://kiraai.ai/platform/kira-on-aws/)
- **Collection/training:** Play's developer declaration says no collection/sharing; website privacy describes account, content and integration data. Privacy/Trust Center say no business-content training; commercial terms retain a separately opted-in contribution exception. Confirm which binding commitment applies. [KI06](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.kiraai.app) · [KI26](https://kiraai.ai/legal/privacy-policy/) · [KI22](https://kiraai.ai/legal/trust-center/) · [KI05](https://kiraai.ai/legal/terms-of-service/)
- **Deletion:** deletion instructions promise completion within **30 days**, whereas privacy allows deletion/de-identification within **90 days** with exceptions and residual backups/logs. Obtain a data-class schedule and downstream propagation rules. [KI27](https://kiraai.ai/legal/data-deletion/) · [KI26](https://kiraai.ai/legal/privacy-policy/)

**UNKNOWN / procurement gates:** executed DPA, inference regions, actual SSO coverage, customer audit export and deletion verification. Different wording may reflect different modules or stale documentation; it is not evidence of misconduct. For sensitive use, resolve these before granting production access.
